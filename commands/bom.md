description: Bill of materials (Master Production Plan) of one or more products
argument-hint: <product code(s)>
---
Show the BOM of product(s) "$ARGUMENTS". In Mirror a BOM is the Master Production Plan: list
materials (with quantity and unit), processes in order with their department, and outsourcing.
Look up all product codes in one lookup call, then use the `production_plan` recipe.
If an intermediate product has its own plan, say so with its plan number.
